If you are searching for the best AI mock interview tools, the most important question is not which tool has the longest question bank. It is whether the tool can listen to your real answer, ask a useful follow-up question, and tell you what sounded risky before your appointment.

For F1 applicants, that usually means school choice, funding, home ties, and post-study plans. For H1B applicants, the tool should probe job role, employer relationship, specialty occupation, salary, and end-client details. For B1/B2 applicants, the best tool should test travel purpose, length of stay, funding, and reasons to return.

## 1. Jiandae

Jiandae is our pick for applicants who want a realistic visa interview simulator across more than one country or visa type. It is especially useful if your risk is not just "What questions will they ask?" but "What follow-up will I get if my answer sounds vague?"

**Strengths:** Jiandae supports common US student, visitor, work, and family-visit situations, plus UK, Canada, Schengen, and Australia preparation. The strongest part is the adaptive flow: the session can probe your visa type, background, funding, refusal history, country context, and answer consistency.

**Weaknesses:** Because the product supports many visa categories, extremely niche consulate-specific edge cases may still require official embassy guidance or professional legal advice. It is a preparation tool, not a visa approval guarantee.

**Pricing:** The public pricing page shows a free trial, $8 one-time for 7 days, and $24 one-time for 30 days.

**CTA:** [Try Jiandae free](/login?callbackUrl=/practice) or start with the [free F1 visa interview practice guide](/guides/us-f1-student-visa).

## 2. Permito.ai

Permito.ai is a strong US-visa-focused competitor, especially for applicants who want speech-to-speech pressure and a product built around F-1, B1/B2, H-1B, and related US interview categories.

**Strengths:** Public pages emphasize real voice, follow-up questions, contradiction detection, red-flag spotting, and plan-specific US visa flows. Permito also publishes dedicated pages for F-1, H-1B, and B1/B2 practice.

**Weaknesses:** The product is highly US-focused. That is useful for US visas, but less helpful if you also need UK, Canada, Schengen, or Australia preparation in the same workspace.

**Pricing:** Permito's public pricing page lists one free mock interview, $19 for 3 mock interviews, $29 for 10 mock interviews, and a discounted $59 one-time unlimited plan at the time checked.

**CTA:** [Visit Permito.ai](https://permito.ai/).

## Which tool for your visa type?

### F-1

Best shortlist: Jiandae, Permito.ai, YMGrad, and MockVisa.

F1 visa interview prep tools should test more than question memorization. Look for school-choice follow-ups, sponsor and funding checks, home-ties pressure, OPT nuance, and the ability to explain why your US study plan is temporary and credible.

Our pick: Jiandae if you want adaptive F1 practice with a free first session and broader visa support. YMGrad and MockVisa are worth checking if you only want F1-focused student practice.

### H-1B

Best shortlist: Jiandae, Permito.ai, and VisaCoach.io.

H1B interview preparation tools need to probe job role, employer details, end-client setup, specialty occupation, salary, and document consistency. A generic interview tool is usually too broad for this.

Our pick: Jiandae for broad adaptive practice, especially if you want to rehearse job facts and document consistency. Permito.ai and VisaCoach.io also publicly position themselves around H-1B practice.

### B1-B2

Best shortlist: Jiandae, Permito.ai, VisaCoach.io, and Atlys.

B1/B2 applicants should prioritize purpose of travel, length of stay, funding, itinerary, previous travel, family in the US, and reasons to return. Free tools are useful here, but the best prep usually comes from follow-up questions that catch contradictions.

Our pick: Jiandae if you want a report and repeated practice. Atlys is a strong free starting point for basic B1/B2-style speaking practice.

### Budget-conscious

Best shortlist: Atlys, Jiandae, Permito.ai, YMGrad, and InterviewBuddy.

Start with free trials first. A free mock is enough to learn whether the voice flow, feedback, and follow-ups feel useful. After that, compare the paid plan against your timeline: a short 7-day plan can be enough if your interview is soon, while a 30-day or multi-session plan makes sense if you need repeated practice.

Our pick: Try Jiandae free first, then choose the shortest paid window that matches your appointment date.

## How to choose from this comparison

Use this simple filter:

1. If your interview is F1, choose a tool that probes funding, school choice, home ties, and OPT without pushing you into memorized scripts.
2. If your interview is H1B, choose a tool that asks about job duties, employer control, end-client details, salary, and degree fit.
3. If your interview is B1/B2, choose a tool that can pressure-test travel purpose, itinerary, funds, and return reasons.
4. If your answers are long or nervous, prioritize voice quality and follow-up questions over a giant static question list.
5. If you have a prior refusal, choose the tool with the clearest feedback report and the ability to practice the same weak point repeatedly.

No AI visa interview tool can guarantee approval. The right tool should help you sound clearer, more consistent, and more prepared under pressure.
